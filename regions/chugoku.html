Chugoku

Where history echoes — peace memorials, floating shrines, and the gentle Inland Sea

Top Sightseeing Spots

Hiroshima

Hiroshima Peace Memorial

The Atomic Bomb Dome, preserved in its post-bombing state, stands as a powerful UNESCO World Heritage symbol of peace. The adjacent Peace Memorial Park and Museum offer a deeply moving account of the 1945 bombing and a message of hope for a nuclear-free world.

Hatsukaichi, Hiroshima

Itsukushima Shrine

The iconic "floating" torii gate of Miyajima Island is one of Japan's most photographed sights. At high tide, the vermillion gate and shrine buildings appear to float on the water. The island is also home to wild deer, ancient forests, and excellent oyster cuisine.

Okayama

Okayama Korakuen

One of Japan's three great gardens, Korakuen was completed in 1700 and features expansive lawns, ponds, plum and cherry groves, and tea fields. The garden offers beautiful views of nearby Okayama Castle, known as the "Crow Castle" for its black exterior.

Shimane Prefecture

Izumo Taisha

One of Japan's oldest and most important Shinto shrines, Izumo Taisha is dedicated to Okuninushi, the deity of relationships and marriage. The shrine features Japan's largest shimenawa (sacred rope), weighing about 5 tons, and draws visitors seeking blessings for love and good fortune.

Tottori

Tottori Sand Dunes

Stretching 16 kilometers along the Sea of Japan coast and rising up to 50 meters high, these are Japan's largest sand dunes. Visitors can enjoy camel rides, sandboarding, paragliding, and stunning sunset views over the dunes and ocean.

Hiroshima Prefecture

Onomichi

This charming hillside port town along the Seto Inland Sea is known for its winding temple walk connecting 25 temples, its cat-filled alleyways, and as the starting point of the Shimanami Kaido cycling route across six islands to Shikoku.

Getting There

Hiroshima is about 4 hours from Tokyo by Tokaido-Sanyo Shinkansen, or 1 hour 20 minutes from Osaka. Okayama is a major shinkansen stop roughly 3 hours 20 minutes from Tokyo. Miyajima Island is reached by a short ferry from Miyajimaguchi, about 30 minutes from Hiroshima. Tottori and Izumo are served by limited express trains and domestic flights.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (late March to mid-April) brings cherry blossoms to the Peace Memorial Park and Korakuen Garden. Autumn (November) offers beautiful foliage on Miyajima Island and throughout the region. Summer is warm and ideal for cycling the Shimanami Kaido. The Hiroshima Peace Memorial Ceremony on August 6th is a profoundly moving annual event.
