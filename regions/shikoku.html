Shikoku

Japan's spiritual island — ancient pilgrimages, hidden valleys, and the warmth of rural life

Top Sightseeing Spots

All Four Prefectures

88 Temple Pilgrimage

The Shikoku Henro is a 1,200-kilometer pilgrimage circuit visiting 88 Buddhist temples associated with the monk Kukai (Kobo Daishi). Walking the full route takes 30 to 60 days, though many visitors choose to visit select temples by car or bus.

Takamatsu, Kagawa

Ritsurin Garden

Considered one of Japan's finest landscape gardens, Ritsurin Garden took over a century to complete. Its six ponds and thirteen landscaped hills are set against the wooded backdrop of Mount Shiun, creating beautiful compositions from every angle.

Tokushima Prefecture

Iya Valley

One of Japan's three most secluded valleys, Iya features dramatic gorges, vine bridges originally built by fleeing Heike warriors, and thatched-roof farmhouses. The area offers rafting, hiking, and a glimpse into a Japan untouched by time.

Matsuyama, Ehime

Dogo Onsen

One of Japan's oldest hot springs with over 3,000 years of history, Dogo Onsen's main bathhouse is an ornate three-story wooden structure built in 1894. It is said to have inspired the bathhouse in Hayao Miyazaki's "Spirited Away."

Kochi Prefecture

Shimanto River

Known as Japan's last clear stream, the Shimanto River flows 196 kilometers through pristine countryside without a single dam. Visitors can canoe, swim, and cross unique chinkabashi (submersible bridges) designed to survive flooding by allowing water to pass over them.

Naruto, Tokushima

Naruto Whirlpools

Created by tidal currents rushing through the narrow Naruto Strait, these powerful whirlpools can reach up to 20 meters in diameter. Visitors can observe them from sightseeing boats or the glass-floored Uzu no Michi walkway beneath the Onaruto Bridge.

Getting There

Shikoku is connected to Honshu by three bridge routes. The most popular access is via Okayama, crossing the Seto Ohashi Bridge by JR train to Takamatsu in about 1 hour. Matsuyama and Takamatsu have domestic airports with flights from Tokyo and Osaka. Ferries also connect various ports on Shikoku to Honshu and Kyushu. A rental car is recommended for exploring the rural interior.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (March to May) is ideal for the pilgrimage and garden visits, with pleasant temperatures and cherry blossoms in early April. The Awa Odori dance festival in Tokushima during mid-August is one of Japan's largest. Autumn (October to November) brings beautiful foliage to the Iya Valley. The Naruto whirlpools are most impressive during spring and autumn tides.
