Kyushu

Fire and water — active volcanoes, legendary hot springs, and bold southern flavors

Top Sightseeing Spots

Fukuoka

Fukuoka Yatai Stalls

Fukuoka's iconic open-air food stalls line the banks of the Naka River and the streets of Tenjin and Nakasu every evening. These yatai serve Hakata-style tonkotsu ramen, yakitori, gyoza, and oden in an intimate, convivial atmosphere that is uniquely Fukuoka.

Kumamoto

Kumamoto Castle

One of Japan's most impressive castles, Kumamoto Castle was built in 1607 by the legendary warrior Kato Kiyomasa. Its ingenious curved stone walls, called musha-gaeshi, were designed to be unscalable. The castle is undergoing restoration after the 2016 earthquakes and remains a powerful sight.

Oita Prefecture

Beppu Hot Springs

Beppu produces more hot spring water than any other city in Japan, with eight distinct onsen districts. The "Hells of Beppu" are seven spectacular hot springs for viewing rather than bathing, including a cobalt-blue pool, a blood-red pond, and a geyser that erupts every 30 minutes.

Nagasaki

Nagasaki Peace Park

Built to commemorate the atomic bombing of August 9, 1945, the Peace Park features the iconic Peace Statue and a solemn memorial hall. The adjacent Atomic Bomb Museum provides a powerful and educational account of the bombing and its aftermath, promoting a message of peace.

Kagoshima Prefecture

Yakushima

This UNESCO World Heritage island is home to ancient cedar forests, some trees over 7,000 years old. The moss-covered Shiratani Unsuikyo Ravine inspired the forest in Miyazaki's "Princess Mononoke." Yakushima receives enormous rainfall, creating lush subtropical and alpine ecosystems.

Kumamoto Prefecture

Aso Volcano

Mount Aso has one of the world's largest calderas, measuring 25 kilometers across. The active Nakadake crater emits volcanic gases and occasionally erupts, while the surrounding grasslands of Kusasenri offer horseback riding and panoramic views of the dramatic volcanic landscape.

Getting There

The Sanyo-Kyushu Shinkansen connects Osaka to Fukuoka (Hakata Station) in about 2 hours 20 minutes, and continues to Kumamoto and Kagoshima. Fukuoka Airport is conveniently located just minutes from the city center by subway. Yakushima can be reached by high-speed ferry or flight from Kagoshima. Beppu and Nagasaki are accessible by limited express trains from Hakata.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (March to April) is wonderful for cherry blossoms and mild weather. Kyushu's warmer climate means earlier blooms than most of Japan. Summer brings vibrant festivals but also heat and humidity. Autumn (October to November) is ideal with comfortable temperatures and autumn colors. Winter is mild compared to northern Japan, making it a great time for hot springs in Beppu and Kurokawa Onsen.
